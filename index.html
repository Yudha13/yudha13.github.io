Field Data & Telematics

Machines, tracks, AB lines, paddock maps and connected data form an operational layer around the physical field.

Data should describe the field, not just the screen.

Field data work checks whether machine information, positioning, mapping and operational records are consistent with what happened. The goal is usable information for planning, monitoring, validation and decisions.

Weather Monitoring

Weather is an operational input.

Automatic weather stations from different vendors can provide rainfall, temperature, humidity and wind observations. The routine is checking whether readings are trustworthy and relevant to field conditions.
